Fast chain.
Faster traps.

Robinhood Chain confirms blocks in about a tenth of a second, and new tokens list every hour. The tricks used to drain buyers move just as fast, and most of them never show up on a price chart.

Hidden switches

The owner can mint more supply, raise the sell tax, or blacklist your wallet after you buy.

Zapify reads the contract bytecode and ownership.

Bundled buyers

A handful of wallets funded from the same source buy in the same block to fake demand.

Zapify traces where recent buyers got their ETH.

Manufactured hype

Copy-paste posts from empty accounts drown out the quiet few saying they can't sell.

Zapify weighs what's said on 𝕏, and who says it.

Two lenses,
one scan.

A contract can look clean while its buyers are coordinated. A token can trend while its holders are trapped. Zapify checks both sides and shows you where they disagree.

On-chain

Robinhood Chain RPC, Blockscout, DexScreener
  • Owner powers

    Mint, blacklist, tax changes, trading switch, pause and upgradeable proxies. These only count against a token while the owner is still active.

  • Liquidity and sells

    Pool depth across Robinhood Chain pairs, plus the pattern of steady buys with no sells that marks a honeypot.

  • Holder spread

    The largest wallet and the top ten, with pools, lockers and burn addresses left out.

  • Funding clusters

    Where recent buyers got their ETH, grouping wallets that share a funder or were funded by the deployer.

  • Launch behaviour

    Buying bunched into one block, near-identical buy sizes, brand-new wallets, and a deployer selling into the pool.

On 𝕏

Cashtag and contract mentions
  • Mentions

    Searches the $TICKER and the raw contract address, because a cashtag can't start with 0x.

  • Weighted tone

    Scores each post, understands phrases like "not a rug", and counts posts people engaged with more heavily.

  • Shill filter

    Discounts duplicated text and accounts with almost no followers.

  • Scam trail

    Counts posts that mention a rug, a honeypot, or being unable to sell.

Hype never lowers your score.

Excitement on 𝕏 is not evidence that a contract is safe, so only warning signs change the number.

      Questions,

      Do I need to connect a wallet?

      No. Zapify only reads public data. It never asks for a signature, an approval or a connection.

      Does a low-risk result mean a token is safe?

      No. A low score means the checks Zapify ran did not find obvious red flags. Contract owners can still renounce later, add liquidity, or change behavior. Always size your positions carefully.

      Why does data coverage matter?

      If an explorer is down or a token has no DexScreener pair yet, fewer checks can run. A score of 10 with 40% coverage is far less conclusive than a score of 10 with 100% coverage.

      Can a token score change over time?

      Yes. As new buyers trade, as the deployer takes profits, or as the conversation on 𝕏 evolves, each new scan reflects the latest on-chain and social reality.

      Which networks are supported?

      Zapify is built exclusively for Robinhood Chain mainnet (Chain ID 4663). Tokens on Ethereum, Solana, Base, or Arbitrum are not scanned.

      How does Zapify analyze 𝕏 (Twitter) sentiment?

      Zapify searches for the token's cashtag and contract address on 𝕏, analyzing post volume, duplicate copy-paste shilling, and scam/honeypot reports with custom crypto sentiment heuristics.
